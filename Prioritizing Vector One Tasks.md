# Prioritizing Vector One Tasks

Based on the context of Vector One Holdings as a newly incorporated integrated deep-tech group (incorporation date: Sept 17, 2026), here are the **5 immediate impactful tasks** to prioritize to establish credibility and gain early traction:

### 1. Launch the Digital Presence (Execute the Implementation Plan)
Your immediate priority is establishing a professional online footprint. You need to build and deploy the `index.html` dynamic website as outlined in your implementation plan. The high-tech aesthetic, interactive features, and clear articulation of your North Star Goal are crucial for first impressions with potential partners, investors, and clients.

### 2. Develop High-Fidelity Prototypes for the Core Demos
The website promises an **EvoMax Token Savings Calculator** and a **C-UAS Telemetry Radar Simulator**. Developing these not just as UI animations, but as functional, interactive prototypes will serve as powerful lead-generation tools and technical proofs-of-concept during early business development meetings.

### 3. Secure Initial Pilot Programs or Letters of Intent (LOIs)
For deep-tech ventures like Vector Aero (defense) and Vector Compute (AI logistics), market validation is critical. Focus on identifying 2-3 strategic partners or enterprise clients willing to participate in a pilot program or sign an LOI for your agentic deployment or aerospace defense solutions. 

### 4. Prepare the Investor & Stakeholder "Data Room"
To fund the ambitious integration of physical aerospace defense and AI compute logistics, you will need capital. Begin preparing your core stakeholder collateral, including:
*   A comprehensive Pitch Deck aligned with the North Star Goal.
*   Technical whitepapers detailing the proprietary tech behind Vector Intelligence and Vector Aero.
*   Financial projections and early business models.

### 5. Formalize the Vector Institute Strategy
Since human capital upskilling is one of your core pillars, start defining the curriculum and operational structure for the Vector Institute. Establishing this early ensures you have a sustainable talent pipeline to fuel the highly specialized needs of your other three deep-tech pillars (Aero, Compute, Intelligence).
