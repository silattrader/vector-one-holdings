# Vector One Holdings — Market Sizing & Research (TAM/SAM/SOM)

This document provides a rigorous, triangulated market size analysis for Vector One's two primary revenue pillars: **Vector Compute** (EvoMax Token Engine) and **Vector Aero** (C-UAS & Radar). The methodology adheres to both top-down industry forecasting and bottom-up unit economic analysis.

---

## Part 1: Vector Compute (Token Distribution & Compute Efficiency)

### Market Definition
The market space is defined as **Enterprise Generative AI Infrastructure, Token Distribution, and LLM Middleware** in the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region, specifically targeting ASEAN enterprises (e.g., PETRONAS, TM) that require localized data sovereignty, high-volume API token access, and cost-reduction middleware (EvoMax).

### TAM / SAM / SOM

| Metric | Current Estimate (2025) | 3-Year Projection (2028) | Method | Confidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TAM** | $2.77 Billion | $6.9 Billion | Top-down (APAC GenAI Market) | High |
| **SAM** | $910 Million | $2.3 Billion | Filtered: Enterprise LLM APIs (APAC) | Med-High |
| **SOM** | $27.5 Million | $85 Million | Penetration Model (ASEAN) | Medium |

### Sizing Methodology

**Top-Down:** 
*   **TAM:** IDC and MarketsandMarkets estimate the specific APAC Generative AI market at $2.77B in 2025, growing at a ~35.5% CAGR.
*   **SAM:** Filtering for just the Enterprise LLM API, tooling, and middleware sub-segment yields approximately $910M globally in the region, acting as our Serviceable Addressable Market.

**Bottom-Up:** 
*   *Target Customers:* 550 large enterprise and government entities in the ASEAN region (Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia).
*   *Unit Economics:* Average annual LLM token compute budget of $50,000 per enterprise.
*   *Calculation:* 550 targets × $50,000 ACV = $27.5M Addressable Pipeline.

**Reconciliation:** 
The top-down SAM ($910M across all of APAC) validates our bottom-up calculation of $27.5M specifically within the accessible ASEAN high-compliance enterprise segment. At ~3% penetration of the SAM, our SOM is highly achievable.

### Growth Drivers & Trends
*   **Agentic AI Expansion:** Enterprises are moving from single-turn chatbots to multi-agent autonomous systems, scaling token consumption exponentially.
*   **Cost Sensitivity:** As token volumes explode, demand for context-compression middleware like EvoMax (reducing API bills by 30-40%) transitions from a "nice-to-have" to a financial necessity.
*   **Sovereign AI Policies:** Regional governments mandating on-shore data processing creates a moat against foreign cloud providers, perfectly positioning localized token distribution.

### Key Assumptions & Risks
| Assumption | Impact if Wrong | Confidence | Validation Method |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Average enterprise compute spend is $50K/year. | If spend is lower, the bottom-up SOM shrinks proportionally. | Medium | Monitor token consumption logs from initial pilot programs. |

## Part 2: Vector Aero (C-UAS, AESA Radar & Interdictors)

### Market Definition
The market space is defined as **Counter-Unmanned Aircraft Systems (C-UAS)**—encompassing AESA 3D micro-radar, RF jamming, HPM interdictors, and kinetic nets—deployed for defense, border security, and commercial critical infrastructure (e.g., airports, oil & gas pipelines) in the APAC region.

### TAM / SAM / SOM

| Metric | Current Estimate (2025) | 3-Year Projection (2028) | Method | Confidence |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TAM** | $774 Million | $1.7 Billion | Top-down (APAC C-UAS Market) | High |
| **SAM** | $294 Million | $645 Million | Filtered: Radar/Interdictor Hardware | High |
| **SOM** | $15 Million | $45 Million | Penetration Model (ASEAN) | Medium |

### Sizing Methodology

**Top-Down:** 
*   **TAM:** Industry consensus values the APAC C-UAS market at $774M in 2025, growing at a rapid ~22-30% CAGR due to geopolitical tensions and border modernization.
*   **SAM:** Hardware sensors—specifically AESA radar and RF effectors—account for approximately 38% of total C-UAS system value, yielding a SAM of $294M.

**Bottom-Up:** 
*   *Target Customers:* 10 major critical infrastructure operators in ASEAN (e.g., MAHB airports, PETRONAS refineries, military forward bases).
*   *Volume & Unit Economics:* Average 5 critical sites per operator × $300,000 per integrated C-UAS AESA/HPM node.
*   *Calculation:* 10 operators × 5 sites × $300,000 = $15M Near-term Pipeline.

**Reconciliation:** 
A bottom-up SOM of $15M represents roughly 5% penetration of the $294M APAC hardware SAM. Given the localized regulatory advantage (MCMC/MAVCOM compliance), securing this market share within Malaysia and neighboring states is a conservative estimate.

### Growth Drivers & Trends
*   **Asymmetric Drone Warfare:** The proliferation of cheap, weaponized commercial drones has forced immediate procurement mandates for AESA tracking systems.
*   **Critical Infrastructure Vulnerability:** Oil & Gas and Aviation sectors (like MAHB) are facing new compliance mandates to secure perimeters against drone surveillance and kinetic threats.
*   **Radar as the Foundation:** AESA radar remains the non-negotiable operational foundation for all C-UAS networks due to its ability to track multiple high-speed swarms in all weather conditions.

### Strategic Implications for Vector One
1.  **Dual-Engine Growth:** Vector One is addressing two distinct, high-CAGR markets (>30% and >22% respectively). This diversifies investor risk while capturing massive enterprise budgets.
2.  **Go-To-Market Sequencing:** The bottom-up models dictate targeting concentrated buyers (e.g., the top 10 infrastructure operators for Aero, and top 550 enterprises for Compute) rather than broad SME spray-and-pray.
3.  **Cross-Pollination:** The software C2 gateway required for Vector Aero's radar networks serves as an internal customer for Vector Compute's LLM agentic processing, proving the conglomerate's integrated thesis.
