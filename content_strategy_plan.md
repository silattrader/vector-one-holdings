## 4. Distribution Strategy

*Budgeting 50% of content effort entirely on distribution.*

1.  **The "Waterfall" Repurposing Method:**
    *   Every Long-form Blog (e.g., EvoMax vs Hyperscalers) is immediately chopped into:
        *   3x LinkedIn text posts (focused on specific stats).
        *   1x LinkedIn Carousel (visualizing the token architecture).
        *   1x Founder newsletter segment.
2.  **Targeted Account-Based Marketing (ABM):**
    *   Content like the MCMC/MAVCOM regulatory guide (Vector Aero) is not meant to go viral. It is meant to be printed out and handed to the VP of Security at MAHB during a sales meeting. Treat highly technical content as sales collateral.
3.  **Community Injection:**
    *   Vector Compute content (Token Compression) belongs on Hacker News, GitHub, and technical subreddits.
    *   Vector Aero content belongs on Defense Tech forums and OSINT Twitter.
    *   Avoid cross-contaminating audiences—distribute specific product content to specific niche communities.
