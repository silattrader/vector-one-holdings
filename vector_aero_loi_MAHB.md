### 1. Purpose
This Letter of Intent ("LOI") outlines the mutual understanding between Provider and Client to enter into a 60-day operational pilot program ("Pilot") to evaluate the **Vector Aero Counter-UAS (C-UAS) Perimeter Defense System**. The Pilot aims to validate the system's detection, tracking, and mitigation capabilities against unauthorized drone incursions within the highly regulated airspace of Kuala Lumpur International Airport (KLIA) or a designated secondary airport facility.

### 2. Scope of the Pilot Program
During the Pilot phase, Provider will deploy a localized, temporary installation of the Vector Aero C-UAS sensor and mitigation suite at a designated MAHB facility.
The Pilot is designed to benchmark and demonstrate the following capabilities against unauthorized aerial intrusions, assisting MAHB in adhering to upcoming MAVCOM perimeter security mandates:
*   **Target Detection & Telemetry:** Validation of AESA Radar and RF Spectrum scanning to detect Class 1 and Class 2 UAS targets at a range of up to 5.0 km from the airport perimeter.
*   **Mitigation Response Time:** Demonstration of the automated Command Gateway triggering MCMC-compliant electronic jamming countermeasures (RF/GNSS denial) in under 100ms upon positive threat identification, ensuring zero disruption to commercial aviation bands.
*   **System Integration:** Evaluation of the C2 (Command & Control) interface and its seamless integration with MAHB's existing Airport Security Operations Center (ASOC) protocols.

### 3. Pilot Deployment & Responsibilities
*   **Provider Responsibilities:** Vector Aero will handle the transportation, temporary installation, calibration, and removal of all C-UAS hardware (sensors, radar, and non-kinetic effectors). Provider will also deliver on-site operational training (1 day) to MAHB Aviation Security (AVSEC) personnel and a comprehensive Post-Pilot Threat Analytics Report.
*   **Client Responsibilities:** MAHB agrees to provide site access, necessary power/network infrastructure for the temporary installation, and coordinate with the Civil Aviation Authority of Malaysia (CAAM) and local authorities regarding airspace permissions for live-testing scenarios. MAHB will also assign a dedicated AVSEC liaison for the duration of the Pilot.

### 4. Commercial Terms Upon Success
Should the Pilot successfully demonstrate the target metrics outlined in Section 2, MAHB intends to enter into a commercial agreement for full permanent installation and ongoing maintenance of the Vector Aero C-UAS system. The anticipated commercial agreement structure will include upfront hardware/installation costs and a recurring annual SLA (Service Level Agreement) for system updates and telemetry analytics.

### 5. Confidentiality, Security & Regulatory Compliance
*   **Regulatory Compliance:** Both parties agree that any active mitigation (jamming/interception) testing will strictly adhere to CAAM and MCMC regulations regarding aviation safety and telecommunications spectrums.
*   **Confidentiality:** All telemetry data, threat signatures, and facility vulnerability assessments generated during the Pilot are strictly confidential and remain the property of MAHB.
*   A formal Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA) is executed concurrently with this LOI.

### 6. Non-Binding Nature
This LOI represents a statement of mutual intent and is non-binding, with the exception of Section 5 (Confidentiality), which is legally binding upon signature.
