# Vector Institute — Enterprise AI Training Curriculum

**Entity:** Vector Institute (A division of Vector One Holdings)
**Target Market:** Malaysian GLCs, Ministries, and Enterprise Multinationals.
**Accreditation:** HRD Corp Claimable (Targeting Q2 2027)

---

## Program 1: L1 & L2 AI Foundations & Practitioner Bootcamp

**Format:** 2-Day Intensive (L1) or 4-Day Comprehensive (L1 + L2)
**Target Audience:** Corporate knowledge workers, HR, Finance, Operations, and middle management.
**Goal:** Transform non-technical staff into proficient "AI Operators" capable of automating daily tasks, securely prompting models, and eliminating repetitive workflows.

### Course Syllabus (4-Day Structure)

**Day 1: AI Foundations & Data Security**
*   **Module 1.1:** The AI Landscape (LLMs, Diffusion Models, Agentic AI).
*   **Module 1.2:** Enterprise Security & Data Sovereignty (PDPA compliance, understanding zero-retention policies, avoiding public LLM data leakage).
*   **Module 1.3:** The Anatomy of a Prompt (Persona, Context, Task, Format constraints).
*   **Lab 1:** Securing enterprise data and crafting the first structured prompt.

**Day 2: Applied Prompt Engineering for Productivity**
*   **Module 2.1:** Advanced Prompting Techniques (Few-shot prompting, Chain-of-Thought, ReAct frameworks).
*   **Module 2.2:** Document Analysis & Synthesis (Summarizing financial reports, extracting legal clauses).
*   **Module 2.3:** Content Generation & Tone Matching (Drafting internal comms, external outreach).
*   **Lab 2:** The "Zero-Inbox" workflow simulation.

**Day 3: Introduction to AI Agents & Automation (L2 Practitioner)**
*   **Module 3.1:** From Prompts to Agents (Understanding autonomous workflows).
*   **Module 3.2:** No-Code Automation (Connecting AI to Gmail, Sheets, and internal tools via Zapier/Make).
*   **Module 3.3:** Building a Custom GPT / Knowledge Base Retrieval bot.
*   **Lab 3:** Creating an automated invoice processing pipeline.

**Day 4: Capstone Project & Certification**
*   **Module 4.1:** Identifying high-ROI use cases within your specific department.
*   **Module 4.2:** Capstone Project Development (Teams build a custom prompt chain or simple agent for their daily work).
*   **Module 4.3:** Project Presentations & Feedback.
*   **Certification:** Vector Institute L2 Certified AI Practitioner.

## Program 2: L4 Executive AI Deployment Workshop

**Format:** 3-Day Executive Intensive (Off-site)
**Target Audience:** C-Suite, Chief Digital Officers (CDOs), VP Engineering, Heads of Innovation.
**Goal:** Equip decision-makers with the architectural and financial knowledge to deploy enterprise-wide AI, optimize compute costs, and manage agentic workforces securely.

### Course Syllabus (3-Day Structure)

**Day 1: Strategic AI Deployment & The "Vibe Coding" Paradigm**
*   **Module 1.1:** State of the Art in AI (What is actually possible in 2027 vs. hype).
*   **Module 1.2:** The Agentic Organization (Restructuring teams around AI agents).
*   **Module 1.3:** The Software 3.0 Operating System (Transitioning from manual syntax to intent-based development).
*   **Executive Briefing:** Evaluating the "Build vs. Buy" dynamic in the era of LLMs.

**Day 2: AI Economics, Token Logistics & Infrastructure**
*   **Module 2.1:** The Hidden Costs of AI (Understanding API pricing, token waste, and context windows).
*   **Module 2.2:** Token Optimization Strategies (Deep dive into semantic deduplication and prompt compression using tools like EvoMax).
*   **Module 2.3:** Data Sovereignty & Security Architecture (Deploying locally in Malaysia, RAM-only processing, avoiding IP leakage).
*   **Case Study:** How a regional telecom reduced their LLM inference costs by 40% while maintaining compliance.

**Day 3: Governance, Risk Management & Board Reporting**
*   **Module 3.1:** Managing the AI "Blast Radius" (Setting up deterministic guardrails for probabilistic models).
*   **Module 3.2:** The Security Paradox (Mitigating vulnerabilities in AI-generated code and automated workflows).
*   **Module 3.3:** Measuring AI ROI (Developing KPIs for AI deployment, board reporting structures).
*   **Capstone Strategy Session:** Drafting the 12-month Enterprise AI Roadmap for your organization.
*   **Certification:** Vector Institute L4 Executive AI Strategist.
