### 1. Purpose
This Letter of Intent ("LOI") outlines the mutual understanding between Provider and Client to enter into a 45-day technical and operational pilot program ("Pilot") to evaluate the **Vector Intelligence AI Agent-as-a-Service (AaaS) Platform**. The Pilot aims to validate agentic automation capabilities within the Client's existing workflows and enterprise systems.

### 2. Scope of the Pilot Program
During the Pilot phase, Provider will deploy a dedicated instance of the Vector Intelligence AaaS platform, featuring a pre-configured AI Agent targeting a specific, mutually agreed-upon workflow (e.g., automated reporting, customer service triage, or internal HR inquiries).
The Pilot is designed to benchmark and demonstrate the following outcomes:
*   **Workflow Automation:** Automation of at least [X]% of the designated manual workflow process.
*   **System Integration:** Successful, secure API integration with the Client's legacy ERP/CRM systems.
*   **Language & Context Accuracy:** High-fidelity performance in regional languages (Bahasa Malaysia/Mandarin) and adherence to local compliance guardrails.

### 3. Pilot Deployment & Responsibilities
*   **Provider Responsibilities:** Vector Intelligence will handle the initial agent configuration, prompt engineering, integration scoping, and deployment. Provider will offer a 2-hour onboarding session and produce a comprehensive "Automation ROI Report" at the conclusion of the Pilot.
*   **Client Responsibilities:** Client agrees to provide necessary API access/credentials to a staging environment (or sanitized production data) and allocate a subject matter expert (SME) to evaluate the AI Agent's performance for 2 hours per week during the Pilot.

### 4. Commercial Terms Upon Success
Should the Pilot successfully demonstrate the target metrics outlined in Section 2, the Client intends to enter into a commercial agreement for a full enterprise license of the Vector Intelligence AaaS Platform. The anticipated commercial agreement structure will include an annual AaaS platform license fee and an implementation fee for additional agent deployments, guaranteeing a 12-month ROI through operational cost reduction.

### 5. Confidentiality, Security & Data Sovereignty
*   **Data Sovereignty:** Provider guarantees that all enterprise data processed by the AaaS platform remains within Malaysia (or mutually agreed jurisdiction) in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).
*   **Confidentiality:** A formal Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (MNDA) is executed concurrently with this LOI.

### 6. Non-Binding Nature
This LOI represents a statement of mutual intent and is non-binding, with the exception of Section 5 (Confidentiality), which is legally binding upon signature.
